Mwongozo wa Rosari ya familia

Mwongozo wa kuchora mistari, kupaka rangi na kusali pamoja. Chagua mojawapo ya makundi manne ya mafumbo kwa Kiingereza au Kihispania, na tenga nafasi ndogo kwa sala ya familia.

Tumia nyumbani, darasani au katika parokia yako.

Sala ndogo. Njia ya kushiriki.

You can begin here before printing. Anyone may name an intention, keep it quietly, or simply listen.

  1. Chagua fumbo.

    For a first moment together, think about the Annunciation: the angel Gabriel brings Mary the news that she will be the mother of Jesus. You can also choose another mystery from the four sets of mysteries.

    Scripture reference: Luke 1:26–38.

  2. Fuata shanga moja.

    With a printed companion, children can trace names and color beads as the family prays. Without a printout, point to a bead on the guided Rosary or follow the words below.

  3. Mwache mtoto aongoze.

    Invite someone to begin one Hail Mary and let others answer. Reading, responding and quiet listening are all ways to take part.

Salini Salamu Maria moja pamoja.

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

Fanyeni huu uwe wakati wa familia yenu.

With younger children
Invite them to point, color a bead with an adult, or listen. No one needs to read every word to join in.
With readers and older children
Let someone choose an intention, read the mystery or lead a familiar response.
With mixed ages
Share the roles. One person names the mystery, another leads a prayer, and others respond or listen quietly.

Keep the complete prayer words beside you with our Mwongozo wa kusali Rosari.
