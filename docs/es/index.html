Sigue el interés y la atención de tu hijo. Ofrece a cada persona una forma sencilla de participar.

Bebés y preescolares

Un momento para dar gracias.

Hold your baby, or sit beside your child. Name someone you love. Invite your child to say “thank you” or make the Sign of the Cross with you.

Jesús, gracias por nuestra familia. Ayúdanos a amarnos unos a otros. Amén.

Let your child name a person, point to a picture, or simply listen.

Reza con bebés y preescolares

Niños de primaria

Let them lead one prayer.

Invite your child to name someone to pray for, or keep an intention quietly. One person can lead and another can answer. Pray this Our Father together, then choose one small way to care for that person today.

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Continue with the guided Rosary

Reza con niños de primaria

Preadolescentes

Bring a real question.

Anyone may share a question or an intention, or keep it quietly. Make room to listen without requiring an answer. Pray this Hail Mary together:

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

Read the Hail Mary and other Rosary prayers

Reza con preadolescentes

Adolescentes

Make space for their voice.

Let each person choose how to take part: name someone who needs prayer, read the words, or listen quietly. No one has to explain an intention. Share a quiet moment, then offer this short prayer:

Dios, acompaña a quienes hemos nombrado. Ayúdanos a descubrir cómo podemos cuidarlos. Amén.

Choose another prayer together

Reza con adolescentes

Start wherever fits your family. With mixed ages, one person can name a mystery, another can lead a response, and others can point or listen. No download or materials are needed for these prayers.
