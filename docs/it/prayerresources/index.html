Un modo semplice per pregare insieme.

Scegli da dove cominciare con tuo figlio. Apri un’attività di preghiera da fare insieme.

Neonati e bambini in età prescolare

Hold your baby, or sit beside your child. Name someone you love and offer a little prayer of thanks. Listening is a way to take part.

Prega con i più piccoli

Scuola primaria

Invite a child to name an intention, lead a familiar response or follow the prayer words.

Prega con i bambini della scuola primaria

Preadolescenti

Anyone may offer an intention, or keep it quietly. Choose a familiar prayer together and make room for questions.

Prega con i preadolescenti

Adolescenti

Let each person choose an intention or a prayer to lead. Quiet listening is welcome; sharing aloud is optional.

Prega con gli adolescenti
Pray the Our Father together

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.
