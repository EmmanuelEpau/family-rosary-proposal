Find a starting point for their age.

Follow your child’s interest and attention. Give everyone one small way to take part.

Babies & preschoolers

A little moment of thanks.

Hold your baby, or sit beside your child. Name someone you love. Invite your child to say “thank you” or make the Sign of the Cross with you.

Jesus, thank you for our family. Help us love one another. Amen.

Let your child name a person, point to a picture, or simply listen.

Primary school children

Let them lead one prayer.

Ask your child to choose someone to pray for. Pray one Our Father together. Let them count the words or beads and choose who leads next.

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name.

Open the complete words and follow a decade

Preteens

Bring a real question.

Ask: “What felt difficult or good today?” Let each person speak without correcting their answer. Choose one intention together, then pray a Hail Mary.

Mary, help us bring our questions and our hopes to Jesus.

Read the Hail Mary and other Rosary prayers

Teenagers

Make space for their voice.

Invite your teenager to choose the time, the intention and how they want to take part. Ask: “Who needs our prayer today?” Share a quiet moment, then choose a prayer together.

God, be with the people we have named. Help us notice how we can care for them. Amen.

Explore an honest conversation about prayer

Start wherever fits your family. Follow your child’s interest and attention. With mixed ages, give everyone one small way to take part.
