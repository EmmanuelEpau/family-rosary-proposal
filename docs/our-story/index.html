Father Peyton and the mission of Family Rosary

Family Rosary helps families discover and grow in shared prayer, inspired by the life and mission of Venerable Patrick Peyton.

“The family that prays together stays together.”

Father Peyton brought a simple invitation to families: make room for prayer together. His work grew into a worldwide mission, bringing people together through the Rosary and the message of family prayer.

That invitation continues in practical ways: a parent learning to lead a decade, children discovering the mysteries, a parish gathering in prayer, and families supporting one another.
