# Family Rosary proposal

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Parents and families with children from babies through teenagers; adults seeking shared prayer, instruction, resources, interviews and listening.

## Product Purpose

Help a visitor begin praying, find a useful family activity or choose something meaningful to watch/listen to. Engagement is a design goal, not a measured result or traffic promise.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Native WordPress block theme and static six-page design-review export. Preserve existing content records, real prayers, source destinations and ranking guide/resources paths. Public proposal releases are authorized; live ministry migration is separate. No fake live status, popularity, credits, testimonials, personalization or collaboration claims.

## Brand Commitments

Family Rosary identity grounded in HCFM.org: Whitney interface and section titles, Calluna editorial/reading, PlaylistScript short accents; HCFM blue, white and yellow. Warm and welcoming to families; do not make the whole experience black. User explicitly requests Prime Video's image-led shelves and subtle ranking, Netflix-style rich title details, automatic featured selection, and distinct artwork for feature/card/detail surfaces.

## Evidence on Hand

content/media.json contains sourced programs; content/resources.json and patterns contain ministry resources. User permits reuse of HCFM/FTP imagery and inspection of signed-in internal-browser accounts. Asset availability does not establish production licensing. SEARCH-DISCOVERY.md records dated Ahrefs keyword evidence.

## Product Principles

Preserve truth and dignity. Give the family the usable prayer/activity on the site. Make the next action clear. Prefer actual ministry people and program frames over generic imagery. Keep all key tasks usable on a phone and by keyboard.
